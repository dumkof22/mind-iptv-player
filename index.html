Frequently asked questions

Does Mind IPTV Player include channels?

No. Mind IPTV Player is a player only and contains no channels, movies or series. To use it, add your own playlist from your IPTV provider (an M3U link, Xtream Codes login or MAG portal). Use it only with content you have the right to watch.

Which playlist formats are supported?

M3U and M3U8 links, the Xtream Codes API (server, username and password) and MAG / Stalker portals. TV guide data is read in XMLTV (EPG) format.

Does it work on Android TV and TV boxes?

Yes. Mind IPTV Player has a dedicated interface for Android TV, Google TV and Android TV boxes, designed for the remote. On TV you can add your playlist by scanning a QR code with your phone.

Can I watch on my TV from my phone?

Yes. Cast to Chromecast and TVs with Chromecast built-in, or send to DLNA smart TVs and media players and control playback from your phone.

Is it free?

Mind IPTV Player is free to download and its core viewing features are free, with ads. Premium is an optional in-app subscription that removes ads and unlocks extra features. Mind IPTV Player Pro is a separate app you buy once, with everything included.
