Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Türkçe

Who we are

Mind IPTV Player is a media player. It does not provide channels, movies or series; you add your own playlist from your own provider.

The app is published on the App Store by Irmak Kurucu and on Google Play by Hakan ER. For any privacy question or request, write to mindiptvplayer@gmail.com.

Stored only on your device

Unless you turn on backup (see below), the following stays on your device and is not sent to us: your IPTV sources (addresses, usernames and passwords), profiles and profile passwords, favorites and lists, watch history and progress, settings, installed add-ons and downloaded files. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Your playlists and add-ons

When you play or load something, the app connects directly to the IPTV provider, add-on or image server you added. Those services see your IP address and what you request. They are independent of us and have their own policies.

Ads, tracking and purchases

iPhone: the app shows no ads and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Subscriptions are handled by Apple; we never receive your payment details.

Android: the free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your advertising identifier. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play; we never receive your payment details.

How long we keep data

A new backup replaces your previous one. Chat messages, reports, feedback, device records and backups are kept until they are removed by moderation or until you ask us to delete them. Usage statistics are kept according to Google Analytics retention settings.

Deleting your data and your rights

To have your backup, Google-linked data, chat messages, feedback or device record deleted, or to get a copy, write to mindiptvplayer@gmail.com with your chat name or Google email. We answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey’s KVKK), you may also have the right to correct or restrict your data, object to its processing, and complain to your data protection authority.

You can sign out of Google in the app’s settings and revoke its access at any time from your Google account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
